# A GSoC Journey

Polaris School of Technology, 5th December 2024

- Rajiv Ranjan Singh

## About

A walk through Google Summer of Code from both sides of the program: what GSoC is,
how to participate, tips for writing a proposal that gets picked, what you actually
get out of it, and where it leads afterwards — drawn from GSoD 2020 with gRPC-Gateway,
GSoD 2021 with Wechaty, LFX Mentorship 2021 with moja global, GSoC 2022 with Keptn,
and mentoring GSoC with Jenkins since 2023.
